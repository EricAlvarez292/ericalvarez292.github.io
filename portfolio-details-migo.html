About

Migo's consumer video app for Indonesia. Users download films and series at Migo WiFi hotspots, then watch them offline with affordable 1- to 30-day passes — no mobile data needed. Key features include catalog and channel browsing, offline downloads, a hotspot location map, passes and promo codes, and rewards.

Re-architected and migrated core features (catalog browsing, content download, and video playback) from Java to Kotlin under Clean Architecture and MVVM, modernizing playback with Coroutines and ExoPlayer — improving performance by 12% and stability by 9%. Also built the Offline Daily Rewards feature, increasing user retention by 20%.
